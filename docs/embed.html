Anywhere

Menu bar, window or widget.

A globe or “LON 14:32” up top, a floating window, and a desktop widget.

On your desktop, too.

Add Meridian as a widget in three sizes. It shows your cities in the same order as the app, with the day and night hours on the large one.

To add it: right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets, then search for Meridian.

Get started.

1

Download Meridian.dmg and drag Meridian into Applications.

2

First launch: right-click Meridian and choose Open. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Meridian.app

3

Click the globe in your menu bar, then Add your cities.

4

Optional: right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets and add Meridian.
