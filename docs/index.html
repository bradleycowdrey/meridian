Install
  1. Download Meridian.dmg and drag Meridian into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click Meridian and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Meridian.app
  3. Click the globe in your menu bar, then Add your cities.
