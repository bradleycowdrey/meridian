# Working days and hours by country

Meridian marks each city's times as inside or outside working hours using the usual office week where the city is. This table is what the apps use (Sources/Meridian/Model/WorkWeek.swift and android/…/WorkWeek.kt carry the same data). Any city can have its own days and hours instead.

It was researched country by country in November 2026 from government and labour-ministry pages, embassies, chambers of commerce, HR and payroll guides and the Unicode CLDR week data. Hours are typical private-sector office hours (start to finish, lunch included). Confidence is high where official or several agreeing sources were found, low where only regional norms or a single listing were (most small territories use the governing country's norms). Corrections are welcome: open an issue.
