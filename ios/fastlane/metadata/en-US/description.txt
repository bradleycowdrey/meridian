Every time zone at a glance.

Each city is a card: its time, large, and whether it's working there right now. The status line under each name says Working, Off hours, Weekend, Day off or the holiday's name, and when the sun sets or rises there. Each card shows its city by day or by night there, on a photo framed on the skyline (111 major cities; others show the sky for the time of day). Five hour tiles in blues run along the bottom, working hours standing out, with a larger Now tile and one outline down every city. Midnight gets a small weekday tag where a new day starts.

Move through time: swipe the ruler and every city moves with you, in quarter-hours of the clock. Tap +1 hr or −2 hr, or Now to come back. Go to any day and time, or type hours from now.

Find a Time: pick a day, a length and who's in it, and see when everyone is working, with the best times first. Add it to your calendar or share an invite with every city's time.

Working weeks for every country: Sunday to Thursday in Riyadh, Monday to Friday in Denver, Ramadan and summer hours where they apply, and national public holidays. Change any city's days and hours.

People and groups: add a card for a colleague in their city, put cards in groups, and show one group at a time.

Clock changes in the next two weeks show above your cities, with how far each city will be from you afterwards.

Search 34,000 cities offline, or an airport code (SFO, LHR), an abbreviation (PST, CET) or an offset (UTC+5:30). My Location adds the city you're in and follows you when you travel.

Widgets for the Home Screen in small, medium and large, each city on its photo with its hour tiles, and for the Lock Screen. They follow your cities and order, and with fewer cities the rows grow to fill the widget.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your cities stay on your iPhone.
