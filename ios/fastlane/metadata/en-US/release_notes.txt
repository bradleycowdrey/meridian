A calmer, clearer design: each city on its own card, time large, and a slider at the top to move every city through time: Now, ±1 or ±2 hours, or type any offset.

My Location: the city you're in is added to your list and follows you when you travel.

Working weeks: each city follows the working days where it is (Sunday to Thursday in Riyadh), and you can set any city's own days and hours.
