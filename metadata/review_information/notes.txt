Purpose and audience: Meridian is a free world clock for the Mac menu bar, for anyone who works, travels or keeps in touch across time zones (remote teams, freelancers with overseas clients, travellers, families abroad). It shows the time in each of your cities as a row of hours, light by day and dark by night, so you can see who is awake and find a time that works for everyone. It is the same app as Meridian on iPhone (universal purchase).

How to use it: No account, login or setup. On first launch, click I Agree on the Terms dialog. Meridian lives in the menu bar: click the globe icon at the top right of the screen to open its panel (there is no Dock icon). It starts with your own city plus New York, London and Tokyo. Drag sideways across any city's hours, scroll, or press the left and right arrow keys to move through time; click Now to return. Click Add City to search (by city, airport code like SFO, time zone like PST, or offset like UTC+5:30); click Edit to remove or reorder cities. The ... menu copies the moment, changes the size and font, opens the panel as a desktop window, and opens Settings (menu bar style, 12/24-hour time, working hours, My Location, open at login). Widgets: right-click the desktop, choose Edit Widgets and search for Meridian (small, medium and large). The widget reads the app's cities through an App Group; right-click a widget and choose Edit Widget to pick different cities.

Location: optional. With Show My Location on, Meridian asks for Location Services once and uses Apple's geocoder only to name the city you're in. Only the city name is kept, on the Mac.

External services: Apart from Apple's geocoder for My Location, Meridian downloads one public text file (its Terms of Use, from GitHub) a few times a day to see whether the terms have changed, so it can ask you to agree again. Nothing about the user is sent. Times come from macOS's own time zone database. Links (bradcowdrey.com, Terms & Privacy) open in the default browser; Share uses the standard share menu.

Regions: Meridian works the same in every region. It follows the Mac's region only for 12- or 24-hour time, which the user can change in Settings.

Regulation and third-party material: Not a regulated industry. City photos are used under their Creative Commons licences, credited in Settings > About > Photo Credits. City and time zone names come from the standard time zone database and GeoNames. No data is collected; no ads; no in-app purchases; no user accounts or user-generated content.
